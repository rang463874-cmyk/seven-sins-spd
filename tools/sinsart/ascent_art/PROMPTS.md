# 역행 보스 그림 프롬프트 (Nov 20 2026)

모든 그림에 공통으로 끝에 붙이는 문장 (배경을 잘라내기 쉽게 하려는 것):

> pixel art game sprite, single character, full body, centered, front three-quarter view facing slightly left,
> clean 1-pixel dark outline, limited palette, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, no text, no frame, no shadow on the ground,
> plain flat solid background of a single color (BACKGROUND), the background color must not appear on the character

BACKGROUND 자리에 각 보스에 적힌 배경색을 넣는다. 다 그리면 `tools/sinsart/ascent_art/<이름>.png`로 저장.

## 악마 쪽

**몰록 (moloch.png) - 배경 #2050C0 (파랑)**
A towering bull-headed demon god of fire, Moloch. Huge curved black horns, glowing ember eyes, muscular bronze-red body,
an open iron furnace built into his chest with roaring flames inside, chains and sacrificial bronze ornaments, holding a
flaming iron flail. Menacing, heavy, ancient idol look.

**릴리스 (lilith.png) - 배경 #40A040 (초록)**
Lilith, queen of the night and first witch. Pale skin, long flowing black hair with a crescent moon tiara, glowing violet
eyes, a dark purple and black gown with bat-like wing shapes, a train of night mist, owl feathers, holding a thin silver
crescent staff. Elegant, seductive but dangerous.

**아자젤 (azazel.png) - 배경 #C04080 (자홍)**
Azazel, the fallen angel who taught men to forge weapons, the scapegoat. Goat horns, tattered dark grey feathered wings,
soot-stained smith's leather apron over dark armor, a halo of floating swords and spears behind his back, holding a
smith's hammer in one hand and a chain in the other. Brooding, sharp.

**아바돈 (abaddon.png) - 배경 #4070D0 (파랑)**
Abaddon, king of the bottomless pit, lord of locusts. A crowned demon with an insect-like dark green and black chitin
carapace, translucent locust wings, a lion-toothed grimace, a golden iron crown, a swarm of small locusts swirling around
him, holding a long pit-black scepter. Plague-like, imposing.

**베헤모스 (behemoth.png, 추격 보스, 더 크게) - 배경 #3080E0 (파랑)**
Behemoth, a colossal primeval land beast. A massive hippo-ox-like body with stone-hard brown hide, cedar-thick tail,
huge tusks, small burning eyes, cracked rocky plates on its back, moss and rubble falling off it. Seen from the front,
charging forward, filling the picture. Draw it big and heavy.

## 천사 쪽

**케루빔 (cherubim.png) - 배경 #208040 (초록)**
A Cherub, the guardian angel of Eden's gate: four faces on one head (human in front, lion, ox and eagle to the sides),
four large golden-bronze wings, bronze armor, holding a flaming sword whose blade is a spinning wheel of fire.
Majestic and strange, biblical, not cute.

**오파님 (ophanim.png) - 배경 #8030A0 (보라)**
The Ophanim, a biblically accurate angel: two interlocking golden wheels (gyroscope rings) spinning within each other,
the rims covered in many open blue eyes, white feathered wings at the edges, a soft holy glow in the center. No human
body.

**아즈라엘 (azrael.png) - 배경 #E0C040 (노랑)**
Azrael, the angel of death. Tall hooded figure in a dark grey and black robe, a pale masked face with closed eyes, large
black-and-white feathered wings, a long silver scythe, and a long scroll of names unrolling from his other hand.
Calm, solemn, not monstrous.

**산달폰 (sandalphon.png) - 배경 #C04040 (빨강)**
Sandalphon, the towering angel of prayer and song, twin of Metatron. Very tall slender angel in white and sky-blue robes,
six light-blue wings, long silver hair, a golden lyre in his arms, glowing musical notes and rings of sound around him.
Serene, gentle but vast.

**메타트론 (metatron.png, 추격 보스, 더 크게) - 배경 #305090 (남색)**
Metatron, the voice of God, an angel as tall as heaven. A gigantic radiant figure of white and gold, thirty-six wings
arranged like a sun burst, countless eyes on the wings, a stern face with a blinding halo, a pillar of light under him.
Seen from the front, filling the picture. Draw it enormous and overwhelming.

## 메모
- 한 장에 캐릭터 하나만. 손에 든 무기도 몸에 붙어 있게.
- 크기는 대충 정사각형이면 된다. 게임용 크기로 줄이는 건 스크립트가 한다.
- 걷기, 공격 같은 다른 동작 그림은 필요 없다. 한 장에서 애니메이션을 만든다.
